Design Studio, Winter 2024

Perth Waterfront B&B

Concept The Terraces

A guest house laid out as a linear-cluster hybrid, stepping down a river slope: the suites and common rooms face the water, and the services face the road.

The brief and the site

A class assignment on spatial organization and circulation. The site in Perth, Ontario, slopes gently down to a river to the north, with a forested edge on the west side and the access road to the south. The programme: guest rooms of 25 m², each with an 8 m² ensuite (five, with room to add three more), an entry, an office, a kitchen, a sitting and dining area with views to the river, an outdoor sitting area, and parking for eight cars.

Sorting the rooms

The first bubble diagram put the guest rooms along the water side, the public space between them and the outdoor space, and the entry, office and stairs on the road side by the parking.

Massing on the slope

Sketched over the supplied site plan, the rooms became a stepped mass between the contours, with the river views to the north and the parking and kitchen toward the road.

The Terraces: a linear-cluster hybrid

The two levels follow the topography. Every guest suite and the common areas face the water; the services (kitchen, office, entry, stairs and elevator) take the less valued parts of the site. The suites step down the slope in two terraced floors, four on each.

The resolved design

Arrival and circulation

Guests park, pass through a small vestibule into the entry area, and then choose: their suites, or the double-height common area, which has the widest views of the river and opens onto the outdoor space on the lower level and the way down to the water. A secondary driveway to the kitchen takes deliveries without disturbing guests at the entrance or in their suites. An elevator and a staircase connect the entry floor to the lower floor, so both levels can be reached without stairs.

The guest rooms

Within the brief's areas, the entry passage leads past a walk-in closet and the ensuite (bathtub, separate shower, double vanity and an enclosed WC) to the bedroom, whose folding glass wall opens onto the terrace.

The river side

The model is a white mass model, as the brief required.
