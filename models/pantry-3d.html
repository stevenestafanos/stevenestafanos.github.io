Second floor pantry / nook

Linen pantry and dry bar, second floor of a private residence. Two opposing runs of SEKTION with ASPUDDEN light grey fronts and BORGHAMN black pulls.

Notes

  • Bottom-run fillers. A-1 and A-4 both print 2⅛ and 2¼ at the ends. With three 24 boxes that is 76⅜, not 76½, and the 2⅛ sits at opposite ends on the two sheets. Modeled with 2⅛ at the interior wall and the rest at the exterior wall. They are cut on site anyway.
  • Fridge fit. 23⅝ wide in a 23¾ bay leaves ⅛ in total at the sides, before any ventilation or door clearance. A-8 lists it as unchecked.
  • Finishes. Counter and niche: Dekton, dark marbled, colour selection pending.
  • Counter items. Ninja DualBrew Hot & Iced (CFP101), 9.94 × 11.03 × 15.22 high, and one cookie jar, shown for illustration. The units in use may vary.
  • Not in the set. Fridge finish.
  • Schematic in the set too. Drawer box positions, fixed shelf height at 30, shelf hole spacing and the toe kick setback.
