Second Floor Pantry

A linen pantry and dry bar designed from site measurements, as an interactive 3D model and a drawing set.

Interactive 3D3D Model Orbit the room, open doors and drawers, switch layers, and view plan, elevations and sections. Drawing set, 9 sheetsDrawing Set Plan, elevations and sections at 3/4" = 1'-0", with parts list and panel cut list. Open in the viewer.
